## Key distinctions enforced in UI and content

- **General eCourts process** vs **MP-specific procedure** vs **court/registry-specific variation** are labelled separately.
- No V1 content asserts a specific MP rule number, form number, fee, limitation period or section number — anything requiring those is marked **Needs verification**.
- CrPC/BNSS transition: V1 deliberately avoids citing either code's section numbers. Users are pointed to India Code + counsel.
