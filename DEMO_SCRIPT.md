## 0:00–0:20 — The idea (Hero)

> “People don’t need another legal search box. They need to know what to do next. Court Navigator is Google Maps for court procedure — MP District Courts, criminal bail.”

Scroll slightly — 3D scales-of-justice motif + pillars parallax behind the headline. Point at the honesty note: *“Procedural guidance only. Not legal advice.”*

## 0:20–0:50 — Setup + Sample Case + Roadmap

1. Click **Start Court Navigation** → Setup shows MP / District / Criminal-Bail locked to V1 (others read “Coming Soon”).
2. Click **Try a Sample Case** → the demo order decodes itself: evidence signals (“why this mapping?”), dates, terms, journey stage updates, checklist re-contextualizes.
3. Roadmap (dark 3D section) lights the timeline, **YOU ARE HERE** moves, progress line animates. Open the stage card: meaning → what normally happens → **What happens next** → sources with links.

## 0:50–1:20 — Checklist + Glossary

4. Checklist: check two items → progress ring animates; point at badges (Source-backed / General guidance / **Needs verification**).
5. Glossary: search “cause list” → plain EN + Hindi + “For you” line. Note: no strategy language anywhere.

## 1:20–2:20 — Decoder + What Happens Next (the killer feature)

6. **Decode a court document** (already running from the sample case, or click a demo doc + **Decode** + upload a PDF to show text extraction) → dates extracted (04-09-2026, 18-09-2026), case ref, terms highlighted, **evidence signals**, structured-AI agreement line, mapped stage, **“What appears to happen next”**, plus *Cannot safely be inferred* list.
7. Scroll to **I am here → what happens next**: branch badge (typical/conditional/order-dependent) + reason + alternatives → prepare → you-may-hear → watch-for → source. Click an alternative branch — the whole page state follows.

## 2:20–2:50 — Trust close

8. Assistant: ask “Will I win?” → refusal redirect. Ask “What does listing mean?” → sourced glossary answer.
9. Scope section: Supported vs Coming Soon + 7 official sources. Close: *“AI explains. Sources establish. Rules structure. Humans decide.”*

## Fallbacks (if anything stalls)

- No network for fonts/3D? Content still reads — 3D canvas is decorative and fails soft.
- Upload fails? Use the built-in demo documents (deterministic path).
- Asked about another court? That’s the point — show the Coming Soon state.
